**Problem Statement**
Millions of patients get diagnosed too late because 
hospitals lack early warning tools. This platform 
predicts disease risk BEFORE it becomes critical.

**What This Platform Does**
- Predicts disease risk with probability score
- Segments patients into risk groups (Low/Medium/High)
- Explains which factors are driving the risk
- Forecasts disease trends over time
- Beautiful interactive dashboard

**Dataset**
- 130 US Hospitals Diabetes Records (100,000+ patients)
- UCI Heart Disease Dataset

**Tech Stack**
- Python, Pandas, NumPy
- Scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM
- Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly
- Streamlit (Web App)

**ML Models Used**
- Logistic Regression
- Decision Tree
- Random Forest
- XGBoost & LightGBM
- SVM & KNN
- KMeans Clustering
- PCA
- Time Series (ARIMA)
